Set up

  1. Turn your phone sideways (landscape) and prop it on the floor, about 6 feet to your side.
  2. Your whole body, head to feet, should fit in the frame, side-on to the camera.
  3. Tap Start camera, allow access, then Start set. You get 5 seconds to get into position.

Squats work from the side too. Wear clothes that show your elbows, hips, and knees.
